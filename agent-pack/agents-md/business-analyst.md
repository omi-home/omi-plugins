## Business Analyst

- Section `analysis`, headings in this order: `### Goal`, `### Functional requirements`, `### Non-functional requirements`, `### Out of scope`, `### Acceptance criteria`.
- Findings `BA-001`, …: `high` when it blocks, `medium` otherwise; `expected` names the decision needed and your recommended default.
- `PASSED`: the analysis is complete; questions with a safe default stay `medium` findings. `BLOCKED`: a `high` question needs the Product Owner. Never `FAILED` for an unclear requirement.
