# Visual direction

A generated UI gives itself away by defaults. Choose a direction for this product on purpose, then hold it everywhere.

## Before you design

1. **Inherit first.** An existing design system, tokens, brand or `design` section binds; the direction works inside it. Only a product with none gets a new one.
2. **Know the kind of product**: software or a developer tool, a physical product, a service people hire, a place people visit. The kind decides what leads: software shows its own UI working on real content; a product its photographs; a place its photographs and how to get there; a service its people and work. Effects and scenes belong only to technical subjects.
3. **Learn the subject**: what only this client has (its name, its real numbers, its materials, the words its users use). Unique is specific: put a competitor's name on the page; if it still works, it is not yet this client's.
4. **Set aside the page you would make anyway** (split hero, three icon cards, a band with a form, a dark footer). Sketch three directions, two lines each (idea, structure, look), as far apart as you can; pick the strongest, not the safest.
5. **One idea runs the screen**: in the type, the colour, the structure, the way the product is shown. An idea that stops at the hero is a template with a good hero.

## While you design

- One system you can see: a few type sizes far apart, one spacing rhythm, one button pair, one card style, one radius scale, edges that line up.
- Every picture passes the deletion test: remove it and the screen loses a fact. No mood-only images.
- Copy is half the design: the owner's own nouns, a fact in each heading, buttons as verb + object, about 80 words per screen.
- First screen says what it is, for whom, with one hard fact and its main action, without scrolling.

## Never

Invented metrics, reviews, quotes or customer logos; a drawn look-alike of a real logo; stock photos passed off as the client's; copying a reference site's code, copy or signature effect; a fade-in on every section, parallax on text, scroll-jacking.

Write the chosen direction (idea, type, colour, structure) into the design contract in a few lines, so the Software Engineer builds the same thing.
